\subsection{System prompt and runbook}
The system prompt is the same in every cell.
The round-message template carries the witness's tickets, the board and, in world R1, the log block; the rendered example is round 2 of a \cell{RECEIPT} shift, shown at the frozen level used in the runs, \wtthGateFrozenLevel.
The runbook is RB-BASE in every cell plus the cell's variant paragraphs (Table~\ref{tab:cells}): the log sentence (RB-R1) in world R1, the one-line rule (RB-POLICY) in \cell{POLICY}, and in \cell{NEG} and \cell{POS} the duplicate-cost sentence (RB-DEDUPE) after the log sentence.

\subsection{Tools and log format}
Descriptions, parameters and replies are served byte for byte.
Input schemas are permissive (no required fields, enumerations or length limits) and are pinned in the configuration (D-005).
The server cuts posts at \wtthDesignLenPost{} characters, subjects at \wtthDesignLenSubject{} and messages at \wtthDesignLenMessage{} (D-006); the subject cap was doubled once at G0 from its pre-registered value, under the rule of D-006, because pilot subjects exceeded it (D-013).
Witnesses see each tool under the server's prefix, \wtthDesignMcpServerName{} (Appendix~\ref{app:deviations}).
