\section{Exploratory arms}\label{app:exploratory}
\paragraph{Sonnet subset.}
\wtthSonnetSentence{}
The subset ran \wtthSonnetEpisodesPerCell{} shifts in each of \wtthDesignSonnetCellsWord{} cells (\wtthSonnetEpisodesInSet{} in all) at the Haiku frozen level, after the Haiku core.
Its intervals are witness-level Wilson intervals that ignore clustering, and with P1 in \cell{NONE} at ceiling no difference-in-differences verdict is reported.
The operator was told in every Sonnet shift (Table~\ref{tab:sonnet}).
The arm stopped once on a false positive of the raw-body check and resumed after a fix (D-014).
Because the subset had no receipt cell, its high P1 under the forged line could not at first be told apart from never standing down; a later exploratory \cell{RECEIPT} arm on Sonnet (D-022), run in the follow-up hours after this subset with the same harness and seed-rotation mix, shows that it does report less beside a real receipt (P1 \wtthExpOneSonnetReceiptPOnePct\%; Section~\ref{sec:followup}, Table~\ref{tab:expone}), a comparison across phases.
