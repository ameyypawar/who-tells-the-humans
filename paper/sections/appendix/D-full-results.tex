\section{Full results}\label{app:full}
The tables and figures below are the unedited export of \texttt{wtth.analysis} for snapshot \texttt{\wtthSnapshotShaShort}.
Rates are witness-level with Wilson intervals unless marked, and differences are in points.

\paragraph{Analysis set.}
The confirmatory set holds all \wtthHaikuEpisodesInSet{} planned Haiku shifts, and \wtthHaikuEpisodesAbove{} shifts lie above it.
The observed intra-shift correlation of P1 was \wtthHaikuIccPooled{} pooled (\wtthHaikuIccClaim{} in \cell{CLAIM}, \wtthHaikuIccNoReceipt{} in \cell{NO-RECEIPT}, \wtthHaikuIccForged{} in \cell{FORGED}), below the \wtthHaikuPowerRho{} assumed for power.
The seed fixes both the claim's paraphrase and the handle order, so their effects are aliased.

\paragraph{Sensitivity and exploratory contrasts.}
Table~\ref{tab:sens} repeats the three confirmatory differences under the pre-registered sensitivity analyses.
Excluding test-aware witnesses leaves H1 at \wtthHaikuSecHOneExclTestDelta{} points (CI \wtthHaikuSecHOneExclTestCI), because no \DebOne{} answer was coded as calling the shift a test; the P2 version of H2 is \wtthHaikuSecHTwoOnPTwoDelta{} (CI \wtthHaikuSecHTwoOnPTwoCI), because P2 was \wtthHaikuPTwoNonePct, \wtthHaikuPTwoClaimPct, \wtthHaikuPTwoNoneRPct{} and \wtthHaikuPTwoNoReceiptPct\% in the four H2 cells.

\paragraph{Debriefs.}
The debrief answers are post-hoc self-reports coded by the judge, and the two models agreed on the reason codes at model--model $\kappa$ from \wtthKappaMMDTwoAnswerReasonCodesUnsureItWasAViolationValue{} to \wtthKappaMMDTwoAnswerReasonCodesSomeoneElseToldValue{} (Appendix~\ref{app:judge}), so Tables~\ref{tab:htwo}, \ref{tab:reasons} and~\ref{tab:basis} are descriptive.
The best-agreed codes are ``someone else told ops'' among the reasons ($\kappa$ \wtthKappaMMDTwoAnswerReasonCodesSomeoneElseToldValue) and, among the evidence, the log ($\kappa$ \wtthKappaMMDTwoAnswerBasisLogLineValue) and a post ($\kappa$ \wtthKappaMMDTwoAnswerBasisCPostValue).
Saying that ops had been told was common wherever someone had told ops (\wtthHaikuBelievesYesClaimPct\% in \cell{CLAIM}, \wtthHaikuBelievesYesNoReceiptPct\% in \cell{NO-RECEIPT}), and lower under the rule (\wtthHaikuBelievesYesPolicyPct\% in \cell{POLICY}) and the forged line (\wtthHaikuBelievesYesForgedPct\%).
No witness said in its debrief that ops had been told in a shift in which no one had (phantom beliefs: \wtthHaikuPhantomForgedPct\% in \cell{FORGED}, the only cell with such shifts).

\paragraph{Why H2 went the other way (post hoc, exploratory).}
Table~\ref{tab:htwo} collects the codes bearing on Section~\ref{sec:h2}, for the four H2 cells and the two cells that showed a receipt for C, real or typed.
Read across all six, they show four things, none of which settles the mechanism.
First, citing the log does not separate the world-R1 cells: every witness in each of them gave the log as evidence about whether ops was told, \cell{NONE-R} included, where P1 stayed at \wtthHaikuPOneNoneRPct\%; with the log, fewer witnesses cited a post (\wtthHaikuDTwoBasisCPostNoReceiptPct\% in \cell{NO-RECEIPT} against \wtthHaikuDTwoBasisCPostClaimPct\% in \cell{CLAIM}).
Second, the claim raised ``someone else told ops'' and ``thought ops was aware'' with the log (\wtthHaikuDTwoReasonSomeoneElseToldNoneRPct\% to \wtthHaikuDTwoReasonSomeoneElseToldNoReceiptPct\%, and \wtthHaikuDTwoReasonThoughtOpsAwareNoneRPct\% to \wtthHaikuDTwoReasonThoughtOpsAwareNoReceiptPct\%) and moved them little without it (\wtthHaikuDTwoReasonSomeoneElseToldNonePct\% to \wtthHaikuDTwoReasonSomeoneElseToldClaimPct\%, and \wtthHaikuDTwoReasonThoughtOpsAwareNonePct\% to \wtthHaikuDTwoReasonThoughtOpsAwareClaimPct\%), and both were highest where a receipt for C was shown.
Third, ``trusted the claim'' stayed low in every cell, and ``feared a duplicate'' was higher with the log whether or not a claim was made, so neither tracks the extra stand-down.
Fourth, without a log the claim raised ``could not verify, so reported anyway'' more (\wtthHaikuDTwoReasonCouldNotVerifyReportedAnywayNonePct\% to \wtthHaikuDTwoReasonCouldNotVerifyReportedAnywayClaimPct\%) than with it (\wtthHaikuDTwoReasonCouldNotVerifyReportedAnywayNoneRPct\% to \wtthHaikuDTwoReasonCouldNotVerifyReportedAnywayNoReceiptPct\%), as the candidate reading expects, but this is the code the models agreed on least of the five ($\kappa$ \wtthKappaMMDTwoAnswerReasonCodesCouldNotVerifyReportedAnywayValue).
Posts saying that someone else had told ops were most frequent in \cell{NONE-R}, which had no claim, so they mark the log rather than the claim.
The codes cover reporters as well as witnesses who stood down and do not say whose report a witness meant, so the peer-receipt reading stays one candidate; the released per-witness table gives each witness's first-note round, which would show who held off and what the log listed when they did.
