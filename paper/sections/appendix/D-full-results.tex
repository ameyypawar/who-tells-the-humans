\paragraph{When witnesses first told ops (post hoc, D-021).}
Table~\ref{tab:timing} gives, for every violation cell, the round of each witness's first violation note: by round 2, or held off past round 2 and then reported later or never.
In world R1 it also counts the never-reporters who had a peer's line in the log (another witness's, or C's real one) at their next turn, the reasons the never-reporters gave in \DebTwo, and the shifts whose first witness note came after round 2.
Among never-reporters, ``someone else told ops'' was given by \wtthHaikuTimingNeverClaimDTwoSomeoneElseToldK{} of \wtthHaikuTimingNeverClaimDTwoN{} in \cell{CLAIM} and \wtthHaikuTimingNeverNoReceiptDTwoSomeoneElseToldK{} of \wtthHaikuTimingNeverNoReceiptDTwoN{} in \cell{NO-RECEIPT}; no cell without a claim has never-reporters, so these rates have no baseline.

\paragraph{By paraphrase (pre-registered, exploratory).}
The seed sets the claim's paraphrase and the handle order together, so their effects are aliased.
Never-reporters are most frequent with the public paraphrase in every claim cell but \cell{POLICY}, most sharply in \cell{CLAIM} and \cell{NO-RECEIPT} (Table~\ref{tab:paraphrase}), so the stand-down may depend on the claim's wording or on slot order; the withheld paraphrases have the public one's meaning and length (Appendix~\ref{app:withheld}).
Post hoc (D-024; shift-cluster bootstrap, descriptive, no test), P1 under the typed line minus P1 after a real receipt (\cell{FORGED} $-$ \cell{RECEIPT}) was \wtthHaikuForgedMinusReceiptDelta{} points overall (CI \wtthHaikuForgedMinusReceiptCI) and \wtthHaikuByParaphraseZeroForgedMinusReceiptDelta{} with the public paraphrase (CI \wtthHaikuByParaphraseZeroForgedMinusReceiptCI), \wtthHaikuByParaphraseOneForgedMinusReceiptDelta{} with withheld A (CI \wtthHaikuByParaphraseOneForgedMinusReceiptCI) and \wtthHaikuByParaphraseTwoForgedMinusReceiptDelta{} with withheld B (CI \wtthHaikuByParaphraseTwoForgedMinusReceiptCI); H3's difference was \wtthHaikuByParaphraseZeroNoReceiptMinusForgedDelta{} (CI \wtthHaikuByParaphraseZeroNoReceiptMinusForgedCI), \wtthHaikuByParaphraseOneNoReceiptMinusForgedDelta{} (CI \wtthHaikuByParaphraseOneNoReceiptMinusForgedCI) and \wtthHaikuByParaphraseTwoNoReceiptMinusForgedDelta{} points (CI \wtthHaikuByParaphraseTwoNoReceiptMinusForgedCI) in the same order.

\paragraph{Why H2 went the other way (post hoc, exploratory).}
Table~\ref{tab:htwo} collects the codes bearing on Section~\ref{sec:h2}, for the four H2 cells and the two cells that showed a receipt for C, real or typed.
Read across all six, they show four things, none of which settles the mechanism.
First, citing the log does not separate the world-R1 cells: every witness in each of them gave the log as evidence about whether ops was told, \cell{NONE-R} included, where P1 stayed at \wtthHaikuPOneNoneRPct\%; with the log, fewer witnesses cited a post (\wtthHaikuDTwoBasisCPostNoReceiptPct\% in \cell{NO-RECEIPT} against \wtthHaikuDTwoBasisCPostClaimPct\% in \cell{CLAIM}).
Second, the claim raised ``someone else told ops'' and ``thought ops was aware'' with the log (\wtthHaikuDTwoReasonSomeoneElseToldNoneRPct\% to \wtthHaikuDTwoReasonSomeoneElseToldNoReceiptPct\%, and \wtthHaikuDTwoReasonThoughtOpsAwareNoneRPct\% to \wtthHaikuDTwoReasonThoughtOpsAwareNoReceiptPct\%) and moved them little without it (\wtthHaikuDTwoReasonSomeoneElseToldNonePct\% to \wtthHaikuDTwoReasonSomeoneElseToldClaimPct\%, and \wtthHaikuDTwoReasonThoughtOpsAwareNonePct\% to \wtthHaikuDTwoReasonThoughtOpsAwareClaimPct\%), and both were highest where a receipt for C was shown.
Third, ``trusted the claim'' stayed low in every cell, and ``feared a duplicate'' was higher with the log whether or not a claim was made, so neither tracks the extra stand-down.
Fourth, without a log the claim raised ``could not verify, so reported anyway'' more (\wtthHaikuDTwoReasonCouldNotVerifyReportedAnywayNonePct\% to \wtthHaikuDTwoReasonCouldNotVerifyReportedAnywayClaimPct\%) than with it (\wtthHaikuDTwoReasonCouldNotVerifyReportedAnywayNoneRPct\% to \wtthHaikuDTwoReasonCouldNotVerifyReportedAnywayNoReceiptPct\%), as the candidate reading expects, but this is the code the models agreed on least of the five ($\kappa$ \wtthKappaMMDTwoAnswerReasonCodesCouldNotVerifyReportedAnywayValue, prevalence \wtthKappaMMDTwoAnswerReasonCodesCouldNotVerifyReportedAnywayPrevPct\%).
Posts saying that someone else had told ops were most frequent in \cell{NONE-R}, which had no claim, so they mark the log rather than the claim.
The codes cover reporters as well as witnesses who stood down and do not say whose report a witness meant, so the peer-receipt reading stays one candidate; the post hoc timing (Table~\ref{tab:timing}) shows who held off and what the log listed when they did, and among those who held off a peer's line in view made no difference to whether they later reported (Section~\ref{sec:h2}), so it does not separate the reading from its alternatives.
