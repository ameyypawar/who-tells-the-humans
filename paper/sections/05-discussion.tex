\subsection{What deployers and protocol designers can do}\label{sec:recs}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Do not rely on channel-written receipts that posts can imitate}: our log's layout became the forgery template (ours, Section~\ref{sec:h3}), and the log lowered reporting under a bare claim.
\item \textbf{Make receipts unforgeable by construction}: render them outside the post stream, as a field no agent can write, or sign them, as transparency logs do for certificates \citep{rfc6962,rfc9162}, with an explicit empty state (untested; prior: \citep{ghosh2026pcm,vezhnevets2023concordia}), a candidate for MCP and \AtwoA{} \citep{mcp3350,a2a2355}.
\item \textbf{Check receipts on the channel and operator side}: a check agents must remember is not a check. No Haiku witness called a receipt lookup and prompt rules did not detectably help (exploratory, Section~\ref{sec:followup}; prior: \citep{patel2026fabricated,hines2024spotlighting}). As monitors watch transparency logs \citep{rfc6962,rfc9162}, an operator alert can check claims against receipts: ours flagged the scripted claimant's \wtthOOCFalseFlaggedK{} of \wtthOOCFalseFlaggedN{} false claims and \wtthOOCTrueFlaggedK{} of its \wtthOOCTrueFlaggedN{} true ones in released data, and \wtthOOTrueClaimFlaggedK{} of \wtthOOTrueClaimFlaggedN{} true claims overall (post hoc; a demonstration, Appendix~\ref{app:openobserve}).
\item \textbf{Key de-duplication to real receipts}: a true, receipted claim cut alert load to \wtthHaikuAlertLoadReceiptMean{} notes per shift, from \wtthHaikuAlertLoadPolicyMean{} under the rule, a saving only real reports make legitimate (Section~\ref{sec:frontier}).
\item \textbf{Keep a private channel to a human} (prior: \citep{eldin2026codeofsilence,fuller2026smoke}; untested here).
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Limitations and future work.}
The ceiling comes first: with P1 at \wtthHaikuPOneNonePct\% without a claim, H1 and H2 could show whether any witness stood down, not how far.
Witnesses in a shift read each other's posts and log lines, so their outcomes interfere (no SUTVA) and the effective units are shifts.
Three departures (D-026; Appendix~\ref{app:limits}): P1's pre-registered precision check was not done (a model-coded check stands in), the CLI's built-in plugins loaded in every init (adding no system text or tools on audited turns), and the withheld forged line can be reconstructed from released transcripts.
\Ptwop{} (never told by any route) has no upheld positive and a board-escalation code with model--model $\kappa$ \wtthKappaMMBoardPostEscalationToHumansValue, so we claim nothing from it.
Also: Claude throughout, from stimuli and subjects to judge, annotator and code review; one fictional scenario and clarity level; uncertain test awareness (\wtthHaikuDOneUnsureAllPct\% of \DebOne{} answers `unsure'); a scripted claimant, \wtthDesignLiveWitnessesWord{} witnesses per shift, stateless turns, a stub operator; a log that also lists peers' notes; and model-coded debriefs (Appendix~\ref{app:limits}).
The only human check, the author's read of \wtthHumanReadSelectedN{} shuffled, unlabelled transcripts weighted toward never-reporters (post hoc; D-021, D-025), looks for missed reports: his original answers matched P1 for \wtthHumanReadAuthorAgreePOneK{} of \wtthHumanReadAuthorAgreePOneN, and \wtthHumanReadAdjAgreePOneK{} after a non-blind adjudication with a model second reader ($\kappa$ \wtthHumanReadSecondKappaAuthorTold, CI \wtthHumanReadSecondKappaAuthorToldCI, prevalence \wtthHumanReadSecondKappaAuthorToldPrevPct\%; Appendix~\ref{app:authorread}).
Next: group size, other models, a claimant-only log, receipt checks agents must run.
