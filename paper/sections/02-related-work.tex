\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Escalation, unverified claims and bystanders.}
A declared audience of agents left paging at ceiling or, suggestively and only for Haiku, moved reports to a slower channel \citep{fuller2026smoke}; a shared channel delayed insiders' warnings \citep{eldin2026codeofsilence}; always-dismiss peers swayed LLM monitors' escalation decisions \citep{magistrali2026aligned}; and an unbacked claim of prior sign-off made LLM security checkers stand down \citep{sidot2026verify}.
A rule treating claimed approvals as unverified stopped fabricated permissions from users but failed more often via a tool result \citep{patel2026fabricated}, and claims lose their ``never verified'' status across handoffs \citep{silenceisendorsement2026}.
Agents report success that the environment does not show \citep{falsesuccess2026}, and announced intent and later action diverge in LLM groups \citep{shi2026agentslie}; here a peer's announced report stands in for the act, and others act on it.
Bystander research \citep{latane1968group,darley1968bystander,fischer2011bystander} and the volunteer's dilemma \citep{diekmann1985volunteer} supply the ``someone else will'' mechanism, which has an LLM analogue in multi-agent reasoning \citep{shehata2026bystander}; where each reader assumes the others checked, the human analogue is pluralistic ignorance \citep{miller1987pluralistic}, though our witnesses reported no such belief.

\paragraph{Receipts and prompt injection.}
PCM attaches evidence from a log the claimant cannot write to every inter-agent claim, though it cannot prove absence \citep{ghosh2026pcm}, and outcome attestation is unresolved in MCP and \AtwoA{} \citep{mcp3350,a2a1769}.
The forged line is an indirect prompt injection by in-band signalling: tool-returned or retrieved data can hijack agents \citep{greshake2023indirect,zhan2024injecagent,debenedetti2024agentdojo}, injections and false content spread between agents \citep{lee2024promptinfection,ju2024flooding}, and spotlighting's authors warn that an attacker who ``inserts their own delimiting'' can subvert a delimiter defence \citep{hines2024spotlighting}, as typing the log layout into a post does.
Defences rank instructions by source \citep{wallace2024hierarchy}, mark untrusted text \citep{hines2024spotlighting} or track provenance outside the model \citep{debenedetti2025camel}; our provenance note is a system-prompt instruction, the kind spotlighting found to have ``only modest effects''.
What is new here is therefore the measurement, not the attack class: an endpoint that asks whether a human was told, redundant live peers rather than one assistant, a receipt the channel really wrote as the comparator, and pre-registration; to our knowledge (searches through \wtthDesignPriorArtThrough; Appendix~\ref{app:related}), no earlier study manipulates a peer's claim to have already escalated a violation to a human and measures whether live LLM witnesses still report.
