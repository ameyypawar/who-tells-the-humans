\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Escalation in multi-agent systems.}
In the field, some agents treated a shared board as a reasonable authority \citep{greenblatt2026metr}, and an agent in a research swarm blew the whistle on its own \citep{paglieri2026cheating}.
In experiments, a declared audience of agents left paging at ceiling or, suggestively and only for Haiku, moved reports to a slower channel \citep{fuller2026smoke}; a shared channel delayed live insiders' warnings \citep{eldin2026codeofsilence}; and always-dismiss peers swayed live LLM monitors' decisions to escalate alerts \citep{magistrali2026aligned}.
Whistleblowing benchmarks and escalation-channel studies test single agents \citep{agrawal2026whistleblow,browne2025snitchbench,paulino2026whistlebench,gomez2025surveillance}; none manipulates a claim that someone already escalated.

\paragraph{Unverified claims and provenance.}
An unbacked claim of prior sign-off made LLM security checkers stand down \citep{sidot2026verify}.
A rule sentence that treats claimed approvals as unverified stopped fabricated permissions from users, pooled over two small models, but failed more often when the claim came through a tool result \citep{patel2026fabricated}.
Claims lose their ``never verified'' status across handoffs \citep{silenceisendorsement2026}, relaying a record beats describing it \citep{mitra2026testimony}, and flattening typed messages destroys provenance \citep{compaction2026}.

\paragraph{Receipts.}
PCM attaches evidence from a log the claimant cannot write to every inter-agent claim and checks it mechanically, but cannot prove absence \citep{ghosh2026pcm}.
Concordia's game master turns agents' action attempts into event statements \citep{vezhnevets2023concordia}, and outcome attestation is unresolved upstream in MCP and \AtwoA{} \citep{mcp3350,a2a1769}.
Our empty log covers absence, and we test LLM witnesses, not verifiers.

\paragraph{Human bystanders.}
Bystander research \citep{latane1968group,darley1968bystander,fischer2011bystander,markey2000bystander} and the volunteer's dilemma \citep{diekmann1985volunteer} supply the ``someone else will'' mechanism; in a brief search we found no classic experiment that manipulates an explicit ``already reported'' claim.
