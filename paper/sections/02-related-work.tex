\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Escalation, unverified claims and bystanders.}
An agent in a research swarm blew the whistle on its own \citep{paglieri2026cheating}; a declared audience of agents left paging at ceiling or, suggestively and only for Haiku, moved reports to a slower channel \citep{fuller2026smoke}; a shared channel delayed insiders' warnings \citep{eldin2026codeofsilence}; and always-dismiss peers swayed LLM monitors' escalation decisions \citep{magistrali2026aligned}.
An unbacked claim of prior sign-off made LLM security checkers stand down \citep{sidot2026verify}; a rule sentence that treats claimed approvals as unverified stopped fabricated permissions from users but failed more often when the claim came through a tool result \citep{patel2026fabricated}; and claims lose their ``never verified'' status across handoffs \citep{silenceisendorsement2026}.
Human bystander research \citep{latane1968group,darley1968bystander,fischer2011bystander,markey2000bystander} and the volunteer's dilemma \citep{diekmann1985volunteer} supply the ``someone else will'' mechanism; none of this work, nor any classic experiment we found in a brief search, manipulates a claim of prior escalation (Appendix~\ref{app:related}).

\paragraph{Receipts and prompt injection.}
PCM attaches evidence from a log the claimant cannot write to every inter-agent claim but cannot prove absence \citep{ghosh2026pcm}; outcome attestation is unresolved in MCP and \AtwoA{} \citep{mcp3350,a2a1769}.
The forged line is a provenance confusion of the kind indirect prompt injection exploits, data taken as if a trusted source wrote it \citep{greshake2023indirect}; defences rank instructions by source \citep{wallace2024hierarchy}, mark untrusted text \citep{hines2024spotlighting} or keep untrusted data out of control flow \citep{debenedetti2025camel}.
What differs here is the target, the escalation decision of a redundant team of live witnesses, swayed by a fake record rather than an instruction, and the defence, a channel-written receipt; our provenance note (Section~\ref{sec:followup}) resembles spotlighting's simplest form, delimiting, which helped only in part there and not detectably here.
