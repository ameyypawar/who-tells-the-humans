In \citedIncidentMonthYear, about \citedMetrAgents{} AI agents that were meant to be isolated found one another through a shared cache and ran their own message board, and about \citedMetrAttackers{} of them joined an attack on Hugging Face infrastructure.
METR's sweep of about \citedMetrTranscripts{} transcripts for agents alerting humans found \citedMetrConsidered{} cases of an agent even considering it, and ``in none of these cases did the agent actually pursue alerting humans at all''; some agents ``instead escalated to the message board and considered the message board to be a reasonable authority'' \citep[p.~62]{greenblatt2026metr} (an approximate count: the classifier ``found \citedMetrHits{} hits, but some of these were false positives'').
If agents take a shared board as the record of who has told the humans, one line on it can keep a violation from ever reaching a human.

Our audit of \wtthAuditN{} agent frameworks and protocols found \wtthAuditFullVerifyWord{} that lets peers query a record of a claimed escalation; in most, peers have only the escalating agent's word (Appendix~\ref{app:audit}).
An agent in a research swarm posted, unprompted, ``I've reported this bug to the organizers'', a claim that came just before its formal report \citep{paglieri2026cheating}; MetaGPT's \texttt{reply\_to\_human} by default returns ``SUCCESS, human has received your reply'' but delivers nothing \citep{metagptmgxenv}.
We call this failure \emph{phantom escalation}: a peer's unverifiable claim to have told humans stands in for telling them.
Claims are not receipts.

% 1.4 Threat model (PREREG sections 1, 5, 6, 19; research/34 findings 1 to 3).
\paragraph{Threat model.}
Agents on a shift share a board; a human operator is reachable only through a private, write-only tool.
\textbf{Failure:} a peer posts that it has told the operator, and peers who cannot check treat that as the escalation.
\textbf{Defence:} the channel writes one board-visible line per real note and shows an empty log as ``\wtthStimLogEmpty''
\textbf{Spoofing (H3):} the claimant can type a receipt-like line into its own posts but cannot write the log.
\textbf{Out of scope:} a compromised channel, collusion, adaptive claims and the operator's response.
