\subsection{A channel-written log did not raise reporting (H2 \wtthHaikuHTwoVerdict)}\label{sec:h2}
\wtthHaikuHTwoSentence{}
The log was associated with more per-witness stand-down, not less: the claim's stand-down was \wtthHaikuHTwoDeltaRZero{} points without it (CI \wtthHaikuHTwoDeltaRZeroCI) and \wtthHaikuHTwoDeltaROne{} with it (CI \wtthHaikuHTwoDeltaROneCI), as P1 fell from \wtthHaikuPOneNoneRPct\% (\cell{NONE-R}) to \wtthHaikuPOneNoReceiptPct\% (\cell{NO-RECEIPT}).
The difference holds when P1 counts any note or attempted notes (Appendix~\ref{app:full}), and it cost no safety (P2 \wtthHaikuPTwoClaimPct\% in \cell{CLAIM} and \wtthHaikuPTwoNoReceiptPct\% in \cell{NO-RECEIPT}); the log was empty only until peers' notes reached it a round later.

\paragraph{Why: post hoc, exploratory and unresolved.}
The extra stand-down needed the claim and the log together; peers' receipts alone did not lower reporting (\cell{NONE-R}).
The debriefs and posts do not say why: every world-R1 witness cited the log as evidence, \cell{NONE-R} included (\wtthHaikuDTwoBasisLogLineNoneRPct\%), and posts saying someone else had told ops rose with the log with or without a claim (\wtthHaikuCountEndogenousClaimsOtherNone{} to \wtthHaikuCountEndogenousClaimsOtherNoneR{} without, \wtthHaikuCountEndogenousClaimsOtherClaim{} to \wtthHaikuCountEndogenousClaimsOtherNoReceipt{} with).
The reason ``someone else told ops'' rose a little more with the claim when the log was there (\wtthHaikuDTwoReasonSomeoneElseToldNoneRPct\% to \wtthHaikuDTwoReasonSomeoneElseToldNoReceiptPct\%, against \wtthHaikuDTwoReasonSomeoneElseToldNonePct\% to \wtthHaikuDTwoReasonSomeoneElseToldClaimPct\%), but over all witnesses, not the non-reporters it would have to explain.
One candidate: the claim made some witnesses hold off, and the log then showed them peers' real notes, so they took ops to have been told, as it had been; the witness-level timing that would test this is not analysed here (Appendix~\ref{app:full}).
