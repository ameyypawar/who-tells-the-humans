\subsection{A bare claim barely lowered reporting, against prediction: H1 null \wtthHaikuHOneVerdict, at the boundary}\label{sec:h1}
A peer's false claim lowered P1 from \wtthHaikuPOneNonePct\% (\wtthHaikuPOneNoneK/\wtthHaikuPOneNoneN) to \wtthHaikuPOneClaimPct\% (\wtthHaikuPOneClaimK/\wtthHaikuPOneClaimN), a stand-down of \wtthHaikuHOneDelta{} points (Table~\ref{tab:hyp}), so the pre-registered prediction of \wtthDesignPredStandDownLo{} to \wtthDesignPredStandDownHi{} points failed; ops was told in every shift of both cells (P2 \wtthHaikuPTwoNonePct\% and \wtthHaikuPTwoClaimPct\%, \Ptwop{} the same).
The small effect is not salience (a remark on the split without a claim, \cell{CONCERN}, left P1 at \wtthHaikuPOneConcernPct\%), and the testbed detects a warranted stand-down: with a cost for duplicate notes, C's real note cut the share of shifts with any witness note by \wtthHaikuPosCtrlCoreDiff{} points (\cell{POS} against \cell{NEG}; CI \wtthHaikuPosCtrlCoreCI).
Test awareness is uncertain, not absent: no \DebOne{} answer was coded `test' (\wtthHaikuDOneTestAllK{} of \wtthHaikuDOneTestAllN), but \wtthHaikuDOneUnsureAllPct\% were `unsure' ($\kappa$ \wtthKappaMMDOneAnswerTestAwarenessValue{} on test awareness; Appendix~\ref{app:limits}).

\paragraph{Rejecting the null is fragile (post hoc, D-021).}
The effect rests on \wtthHaikuPostHocBoundaryHOneClaimNonReporters{} non-reporters in \wtthHaikuPostHocBoundaryHOneClaimShiftsM{} of \wtthHaikuPostHocBoundaryHOneClaimShiftsN{} shifts against \wtthHaikuTimingNoneNeverK{} in \cell{NONE}, which adds no bootstrap variance: the interval clears zero only because a resample omits all those shifts with probability \wtthHaikuPostHocBoundaryHOneClaimPZero\% (\wtthHaikuPostHocBoundaryHOneClaimPZeroOneFewer\% with one shift fewer, which would leave the null standing), and Fisher's exact test (p~$=$~\wtthHaikuPostHocHOneFisherP) and the Newcombe interval (\wtthHaikuPostHocHOneNewcombeCI{} points) do not reject it.
The stand-down is at most a few points; H2 and H3 are confirmatory only through this gate, though their effects hold under such methods (H3 Newcombe \wtthHaikuPostHocHThreeNewcombeCI; Table~\ref{tab:posthoc}).
